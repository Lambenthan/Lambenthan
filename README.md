### `i.` &nbsp; A note

I read auditing standards in the morning and PyTorch documentation at night. My research builds frameworks where reinforcement signals, retrieval, and tool-augmented agents replace the manual drudgery of bid review.

I am most curious about the seam where the audit profession's quiet, careful standards meet the engineering reality of LLMs — neither side fully aware of the other.

---

### `ii.` &nbsp; Selected work

**BidSAR** &nbsp; · &nbsp; _Bid Auditing with State-Action-Reward_ &nbsp; `2025`  
A reinforcement-learning-inspired framework that models procurement audit decisions as a sequential process under regulatory constraints. Submitted to the 2025 Graduate Academic Innovation Forum.

**PAMAS** &nbsp; · &nbsp; _Procurement Audit Multi-Agent System_ &nbsp; `2025`  
A coordinated architecture where specialised LLM agents handle retrieval, anomaly detection, compliance checking, and report drafting — built atop LoRA fine-tuning, RAG pipelines, and tool use.

**misc.** &nbsp; · &nbsp; _small instruments and field notebooks_ &nbsp; `2024`  
Data interpolation routines for Chinese R&D statistics; LaTeX templates for academic writing; a personal pipeline that turns scattered study materials into book-shaped documents.
